# Creative Quality Scoring Methodology & Rubric
## MySivi Ad Intelligence & Creative Analysis System

```
+-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
|                                100-POINT SCORING RUBRIC                                 |
+-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
| Criteria                     | Weight | Evaluation Focus                                |
|------------------------------|--------|-------------------------------------------------|
| 1. Hook Effectiveness        |  20%   | Stopping power, emotional intrigue, stakes      |
| 2. Message Clarity           |  15%   | Immediate understanding of core proposition     |
| 3. Value Proposition & USP   |  15%   | Concrete contrast against manual/template tools |
| 4. Target Audience Relevance |  10%   | Resonance with e-commerce, media buyers, teams  |
| 5. Trust & Social Proof      |  10%   | Verifiable evidence, quotes, statistics         |
| 6. CTA Effectiveness         |  10%   | Action clarity, urgency, friction level         |
| 7. Creative Execution        |  10%   | Copy rhythm, visual hierarchy, readability      |
| 8. Differentiation           |  10%   | Separation from generic Canva/template tools    |
|------------------------------|--------|-------------------------------------------------|
| TOTAL                        |  100%  | Weighted Mathematical Sum (0 - 100)             |
+-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
```

---

## 1. Mathematical Calculation Formula

Every advertisement is evaluated across each of the 8 criteria on a continuous **0.0 to 10.0 scale**.
The total score is calculated deterministically using the formula:

$$\text{Total Score} = \frac{(h \times 20) + (m \times 15) + (v \times 15) + (t \times 10) + (s \times 10) + (c \times 10) + (e \times 10) + (d \times 10)}{10}$$

Where:
- $h$ = Hook Effectiveness (Weight: 20)
- $m$ = Message Clarity (Weight: 15)
- $v$ = Value Proposition and USP (Weight: 15)
- $t$ = Target Audience Relevance (Weight: 10)
- $s$ = Trust and Social Proof (Weight: 10)
- $c$ = CTA Effectiveness (Weight: 10)
- $e$ = Creative Execution (Weight: 10)
- $d$ = Differentiation (Weight: 10)

Maximum possible score = **100.0**. Minimum possible score = **0.0**.

## 2. Detailed Scoring Standards by Criterion

### 1. Hook Effectiveness (Weight: 20%)
- **9.0 - 10.0:** Exceptional feed stopping power. Uses bold pattern-interrupts, quantifiable claims (e.g. "2 minutes flat", "$500+ every month"), or intense curiosity.
- **7.0 - 8.9:** Clear value statement or relatable pain point, but follows standard direct-response formatting.
- **0.0 - 6.9:** Passive, generic, or absent opening hook.

### 2. Message Clarity (Weight: 15%)
- **9.0 - 10.0:** Crystal clear within 3 seconds of reading. Zero technical jargon or confusing claims.
- **7.0 - 8.9:** Understandable offer, but requires reading 2-3 sentences to grasp the full product mechanism.
- **0.0 - 6.9:** Vague, confusing, or contradictory messaging.

### 3. Value Proposition & USP (Weight: 15%)
- **9.0 - 10.0:** Clear, defensible advantage (e.g. editable vector layers, generative composition without recycled templates).
- **7.0 - 8.9:** Strong benefit stated, but resembles standard AI claims.
- **0.0 - 6.9:** Weak or undefined value proposition.

### 4. Target Audience Relevance (Weight: 10%)
- **9.0 - 10.0:** Speaks directly to the specific problems of e-commerce founders, performance media buyers, or agency creative leads.
- **7.0 - 8.9:** Broad appeal to general business owners.
- **0.0 - 6.9:** Misaligned or undefined target demographic.

### 5. Trust & Social Proof (Weight: 10%)
- **STRICT GRADING RULE:** Do NOT award high trust scores to unverified marketing claims.
- **9.0 - 10.0:** Features third-party customer case studies, client logos, G2 ratings, or verifiable metric lifts.
- **6.0 - 7.5:** Mentions user counts or general adoption metrics without third-party validation.
- **4.0 - 5.5:** Copy relies solely on self-asserted brand capability claims (standard for most early-stage ads).

### 6. CTA Effectiveness (Weight: 10%)
- **9.0 - 10.0:** Low-friction, high-incentive call to action ("Try For Free", "Generate in 2 Mins").
- **7.0 - 8.9:** Standard direct CTA ("Learn More", "Sign Up").
- **0.0 - 6.9:** High-friction or absent CTA ("Contact Sales", "Unavailable").

### 7. Creative Execution (Weight: 10%)
- **9.0 - 10.0:** Excellent copy structure, scannable formatting, strong rhythm, and clean visual integration.
- **7.0 - 8.9:** Solid readable text with minor formatting room for improvement.
- **0.0 - 6.9:** Cluttered or dense wall of text.

### 8. Differentiation (Weight: 10%)
- **9.0 - 10.0:** Explicitly attacks competitor limitations (e.g. calling out Canva's recycled templates or freelance designer turnaround delays).
- **7.0 - 8.9:** Mentions AI capability without direct competitive positioning.
- **0.0 - 6.9:** Indistinguishable from dozens of generic AI graphic generator ads.

## 3. Creative Quality Ranking vs. Observable Performance Ranking

In accordance with strict professional ethics:
- **Creative Quality Ranking:** Measures copy psychology, messaging hierarchy, hook stopping power, and strategic positioning.
- **Observable Performance Ranking:** Based strictly on verifiable public data. Meta Ad Library intentionally withholds conversion, CPA, ROAS, and revenue data. Therefore, the top-ranked creative is designated as the **"Best Creative Advertisement"** based on creative excellence, without making unfounded commercial revenue claims.

---

## 4. Multi-Tier Tie-Breaking Hierarchy & Joint Winner Policy

To prevent arbitrary ranking when two or more advertisements achieve identical total scores (e.g. 81.0/100):

1. **Primary Sort:** Total Mathematical Score (100-Point Weighted Rubric).
2. **Secondary Tie-Breaker (Weight: 20%):** Hook Effectiveness score.
   - *Rationale:* Hook stopping power is the single largest algorithmic determinant of scroll-stop rate and 3-second feed retention on Meta.
3. **Tertiary Tie-Breaker (Weight: 15%):** Value Proposition & USP score.
   - *Rationale:* Core benefit clarity drives post-click conversion intent once stopping power is achieved.
4. **Quaternary Tie-Breaker (Weight: 15%):** Message Clarity score.
5. **Joint Winners Policy (Vernacular Parity):**
   - If two or more advertisements share identical total scores and identical tie-breaking criteria (delta = 0.00), the system formally declares **Joint Rank 1 Co-Winners**.
   - Both receive `rank = 1` and `is_best_creative = True`. The next non-tied ad receives standard competition ranking (e.g., Rank 3 or 4).
   - *Marketing Rationale:* When regional variants (e.g., Marathi vs Bengali vs Hindi) achieve equal strategic potency with identical pricing anchors (₹1 trial), declaring joint winners objectively honors vernacular parity rather than inventing arbitrary favoritism.
